Our Days of Fellowship

Gather with us every week as we study the Word of God and experience His transforming power.

Every Sunday

The Sunday Light

Time: 8:30 AM

Our vibrant weekly Sunday worship service divided into two enriching sections:

  • Section 1 — Bible Discourse: Interactive examination and practical understanding of God's Word.
  • Section 2 — The Sunday Light Message: Anointed preaching that illuminates your path for the week ahead.
Every Tuesday

Bible Study (Expository)

Midweek Word & Teaching

A dedicated time of digging deep into the Word of God. Through verse-by-verse expository teaching, we gain spiritual understanding and nourishment by eating directly from His Word.

Every Friday

The Charismatic Hour

Time: 6:00 PM

A dynamic atmosphere of prayer and revival where God changes situations around, turns stones into pillars, heals the sick, and transforms lives. Experience renewal, deliverance, and divine intervention.
